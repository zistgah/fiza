## 1. What Fiza is
**Fiza** (فضا, "air / atmosphere / environment") is a research **proposal** to let scientists,
practitioners and communities contribute to a shared, versioned, falsifiable environmental
knowledge **manifold** — *without writing code* — by extending the Verification-Gated
Human–AI Co-Development (VGC) method into the environment domain. It mirrors VGC-Health
(doi:10.5281/zenodo.21303401): natural-language contributions, a **multi-oracle gate**,
non-collapsing axes, honest deferral, and a provenance ledger — retargeted from clinic to
climate/ecology. Element-named sibling of **zamin** (earth) and **aab** (water). Sprint 0 is
a scaffold; the full paper is Sprint 1.
