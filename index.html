Fiza فضا — VGC for the environment

The environmental replica of VGC-Health: verification-gated human–AI co-development, retargeted from clinic to climate and ecology.

Abhishek Choudhary · AyeAI · sibling of zamin (earth) & aab (water)

Intent (on record)

Let environmental scientists, practitioners and communities contribute to a shared, versioned, falsifiable environmental knowledge manifold — without writing code — where every contribution is gated before it is trusted. The method is proven in health (VGC-Health); Fiza retargets it to the environment.

Status

This is a scaffold. The full proposal is built incrementally under the gate — see the backlog. First open task: fix the three non-collapsing environment axes with the author.
